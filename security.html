Security & Privacy

Security and privacy are the product.

We are an IT and network security team first. Every private AI system we install is built to the same written security baseline, so your data stays on your premises and under your control.

Stays on site

Prompts, documents, and answers are processed on hardware in your building. Nothing is sent to a public AI service.

Never trains others' models

Your data is not used to improve anyone else's AI. The models run locally and learn nothing you don't give them.

You stay in control

You decide who can use it, which documents it can see, and how long history is kept. Your data stays yours at the end of any contract.

Our Security Baseline

What every installation includes.

AreaWhat we do
NetworkThe AI system sits on its own network segment (VLAN) behind firewall rules that block it from sending data out to the internet.
AccessStaff sign in with your existing business accounts. Access to documents follows who is allowed to see them, so one client's file does not answer another's question.
EncryptionStorage is encrypted at rest, and connections inside your office are encrypted in transit.
AuditSign-ins and usage are logged so you can see who used the system and when.
BackupsEncrypted backups of settings and the document index, with tested restores.
PatchingOperating system, AI software, and models are updated on a regular schedule.
Our accessRemote support uses a single logged channel that you approve. We do not keep open-ended access to your system.

Cloud AI vs. Private AI

Know where your data goes.

Business cloud AI plans can be a good fit for many companies, and we will tell you honestly when one is. Private AI is for organizations that need their data physically under their control: confidential client files, restricted contracts, or simply a policy of not sending sensitive information to third parties.

Our systems are designed to keep data on your premises. Meeting a specific regulation (for example HIPAA) depends on your full environment and policies; we work alongside your compliance advisor and will document our part in your service agreement.
