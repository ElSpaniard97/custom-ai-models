Why Private AI

The power of AI without handing over your files.

Most AI tools send your prompts and documents to someone else's data center. For firms that hold confidential client records, contracts, or restricted information, that is a risk many owners will not take.

Our systems run entirely on hardware in your building. Questions, documents, and answers are processed locally, on equipment you control, behind your firewall.

Security first

Built and hardened by an IT and network security team: separate network segment, encrypted storage, sign-in with your existing accounts, and a log of who used what.

Privacy by design

Your documents are never uploaded to a public AI service and never used to train anyone else's model. The system is designed so nothing leaves your premises.

Fully managed

We install it, connect it to your files, train your team, and keep it patched, backed up, and monitored. You get the results without becoming an AI department.
